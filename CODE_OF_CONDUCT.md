# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SEMANTICA project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
showing empathy and kindness, respecting differing viewpoints and experiences,
giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes,
and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or
insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private information
without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer using the maintainer
contact listed in `DESCRIPTION`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly
as possible, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.
